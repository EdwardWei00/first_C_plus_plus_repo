Physical keys are easily lost, stolen, or risk of duplicated by someone without permission. Making a new replacements are costly and tedious.To solve this issue, modern homes and offices use electronic digital keypads. However, basic digital keypads have their own security weaknesses. People often enter a room and forget to lock the door behind them or they get stuck outside which requires specialized tools and factory resets just to update the passcode. This is exactly our system eliminates these vulnerabilities by providing secure and automated access with effortless administration.
